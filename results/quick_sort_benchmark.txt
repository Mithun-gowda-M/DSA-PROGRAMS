Key Empirical Insights:
1. Random Data: Quick Sort is typically faster than Merge Sort due to smaller
   constant factors, cache locality, and lack of auxiliary array copying.
2. Already Sorted / Reverse Sorted: With last element as pivot, Quick Sort suffers
   worst-case partitioning O(n^2), causing dramatic slowdown compared to Merge Sort's
   steady O(n log n) performance!
